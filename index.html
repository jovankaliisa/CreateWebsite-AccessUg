ABOUT ACCESS UG SCH

Who We Are

Making school discovery simpler for learners, parents and communities.

Our Platform

Access UG Sch is a school-focused digital platform created to bring useful education information together in one easy-to-use place. We help users discover schools and learn more about education opportunities.

✓Discover schools across Uganda
✓Explore useful school information

Why Access UG Sch?

Finding the right school can take time. Access UG Sch is designed to organize school information in a clear, accessible and modern digital platform.
